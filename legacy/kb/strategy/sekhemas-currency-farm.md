## Three profit centres (creator's claim)
- Claim: (1) jewels from chests, (2) relics that only matter inside this trial, (3) ascendancy carries. Creator's own league split: ~30% normal jewels/Timeless jewels/relics, ~40% from running the Against the Darkness unique jewel, ~30% carries (front-loaded, drop off later).

## Jewels
- Four jewel kinds drop: common ruby/emerald/sapphire; normal Timeless jewels (gold Timeless chests, need a gold key, from floor 2); Against the Darkness (unique Timeless jewel); Grand Spectrum (unique, ruby/emerald/sapphire variants, ~1 per 10–20 runs).
- Sorting rules: (a) perfectly rolled mods good to fracture for current builds (example: sapphire +20% spell crit bonus sells 11–12 div unfractured); (b) already-good 3–4 mod jewels, best early league; (c) double-crit emerald/sapphire for the suffix gamble (45 div uncrafted, example). Reforge the leftover trash: from 0.5, reforging a ruby + emerald + sapphire gives a diamond, which can roll mods from all three pools (e.g. +max rage diamond ~49 div unfractured). Increased effect of notables on a Timeless diamond ~19 div.

## Against the Darkness (ATD)
- Claim: Drops only from a run using the Desperate Alliance Vaal relic (consumed each run). Two random mods from a pool of 21 (211 combinations stated). Creator quotes: spirit + gain-cold ~2,000 div; spirit + any other ≥100 div; any double "gain" >200 div. Early in his league a Vaal relic cost 12 div vs ATD spirit ~50 div and double gain ~140 div — at those prices he sold relics instead of running.
- Rule of thumb from the creator: farming ATD depends on relic cost vs ATD sale value; watch for relic price-fixing.
- Source: transcript, tier 5 | Confidence: single-source | Volatility: volatile

## Relics
- Non-unique relics: small (seal, urn), medium (tapestry, amphora), large (coffer, incense, vase). Ignore large. Medium worth keeping: boss takes increased damage, extra merchant choice, merchant price reduction, honour resistance (combos ~4 to 30 div, trending to merchant choice later in a league). Small relics matter for the "increased quantity of relics dropped" mod: 0% = 1 unique relic from the final boss, 234% (max) = 3 guaranteed + chance of a 4th. Best paired prefixes: movement speed (double T1 ~65 div), reduced trap damage (~18 div), global defences (~7–8 div).
- Unique relics: Last Flame (jackpot; ~7,500 div quoted, very rare); Desperate Alliance (~15% per relic slot, ~4 div); Burden of Leadership (very common; drops a Sekhemet's Resolve ring; hides the trial view, hurting farm efficiency; ~1-in-3 chance of the useful ring variant).
- Source: transcript, tier 5 | Confidence: single-source | Volatility: volatile

## Ascendancy carries
- Claim: After clearing to the boss, invite up to 5 players (party max 6) to access the ascendancy altar. Creator's example: 4 runs/h × 5 slots × 3 div = 60 div/h before loot, strongest early in a league. Pay-after-service is the community norm; he asks buyers to show currency first because scams happen.
