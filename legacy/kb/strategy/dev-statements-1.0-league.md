## Structure of the mechanic
- Claim: Storyline part of the league has 3 faction castles. After the first you get your first independent commander; the other two come after the pinnacle boss. Everything past the pinnacle (player-vs-player castle/faction endgame) is meant for the top end of players; Jonathan expects most players to stop at the pinnacle boss.

- Source: Interview A and Interview B (both Jonathan), tier 1 via transcript, ingested 2026-11-17
- Confidence: confirmed (two interviews agree)
- Notes: The castle-quest line is the fixed-atlas part; PvP castle layer is "more of a high-level thing" (Interview B). → [[endgame/conquerors-league]] (todo)

## Troops, commanders, gold
- Claim: A castle you build generates troops over real time. Neutral monsters you meet while mapping can be recruited (examples named: elephants, chimeras). Commanders are fed troops; the intent is that castle output roughly matches the commanders you run, and extra recruits from mapping fund sieges and other battles. Commanders keep pathing while you are offline, giving a floor of progress for low-playtime players.
- Gold: Jonathan says battles should supply enough gold to hire troops for the next battle, so the league should not add extra gold squeeze on top of the campaign's existing one. Balance not final.
- Version: 1.0 | Volatility: volatile
- Source: Interview A (Jonathan), tier 1 via transcript, 2026-11-17
- Confidence: single-source
- Notes: [[economy/gold]] already notes Conquerors troops cost campaign gold (launch brief). Interview B: some currency sinks inside the league are being considered ("not fully committed").

## Factions and castles
- Claim: You join a faction; every castle your faction holds can be used as a starting point for mapping and exploring (teleport). Two castles from different factions beside each other siege each other, so troop numbers slowly fall; more castles around an enemy castle makes it fall faster. Each time a castle changes hands its hunting-ground maps reset and can be run again; the juice on those is meant to be high enough to be worth logging in for. Castle bonuses last only while your faction holds the castle.
- Faction switching: allowed, but a cost is not designed yet (rebuying all upgrades was rejected as too punitive). Cost per additional castle rises with the number the faction holds; defecting to a smaller faction to retake a castle is rewarded. Jonathan said GGG cannot QA this at scale ("literally no way") — expect balance changes live.
- Version: 1.0 | Volatility: volatile
- Source: Interview A (Jonathan), tier 1 via transcript, 2026-11-17
- Confidence: single-source
- Notes: Bonus may need an SSF adjustment (raised in Q&A, not designed).

## Atlas changes
- Claim: Starting a quest line now lets you teleport straight to its start. Early-tier (T1–5) odds of the initial league encounters are reduced so new players aren't spammed; ripping through tiers behaves as before. Atlas tree size is being reduced. The new mechanic sits where Abyss used to be on the atlas (Abyss moved). Number of maps to complete last league's (garbled "Runes of Aether"; likely Runes of Aldur) mechanic is being cut for 1.0.

- Claim: Pinnacle bosses can now be repeated without another key (changed since 0.1), so players attempt them earlier. No Uber bosses until GGG is confident in balance: too-hard Ubers limit which builds can finish them.
