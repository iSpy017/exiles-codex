# League Lifecycle Pricing — Lessons

## Late-league spike in low-level uncut spirit gems
- Claim: Late in a league, low-level uncut spirit gems rise in price — few fresh characters are farming them, but alt-starters still want early spirit for fast levelling.
- Version: 0.5.5 (Forbidden Rites)
- Volatility: volatile
- Source: user's own poe.ninja analysis, Forbidden Rites, 2026-11-17 (tier 4)
- Confidence: single-source
- Data (1 div ≈ 520 ex):
  - L8 spirit: ~12.2 div now; 14.2 div 7-day avg; 7.3 div three weeks earlier; ~2.7 traded/hr
  - L9 spirit: 7.0 div now; 4.3 div three weeks earlier
  - L10: 0.44 div · L12: 0.50 div
- Play: sell the campaign L8, buy an L10 or L12 once level allows → nets ~11 div.
- Level requirements (resolved 2026-11-17, see [[gems/gem-levels]]): L8 gem needs char level 26 · L9 31 · L10 36 · L12 46 (PoE2DB level table, 0.5.x). So the swap works from char 36 (L10) or 46 (L12).
- [INFERRED] Why L8 specifically: spirit gem tiers are 4/8/14 ([[gems/uncut-gems]]), so L8 is the cheapest uncut that unlocks the tier-8 persistents (e.g. Blink, Defiance Banner) — a likely driver of alt-levelling demand.

## Crafting currency above 1 ex late-league
- Claim: With Exalted Orbs as the low unit late-league, several basic crafting currencies traded above 1 ex each.
- Version: 0.5.5 (Forbidden Rites)
- Volatility: volatile
- Source: user's poe.ninja data, Forbidden Rites, 2026-11-17 (tier 4)
- Confidence: single-source
- Data: Armourer's Scrap ~8.8 ex · Blacksmith's Whetstone ~6.5 ex · Glassblower's Bauble ~11.6 ex · Gemcutter's Prism ~26.5 ex · Orb of Chance ~24 ex
- Lesson: check the exchange before spending drops on levelling gear.

## Reading poe.ninja
- Claim: poe.ninja's volume figure is per hour, not a cumulative total — a low number may still be a liquid market, and vice versa; don't misread thin markets.
- Claim: The Currency Exchange order book (open buy/sell orders) is visible only in game; poe.ninja shows completed trades only.
- Version: 0.5.5
- Volatility: may-change
- Source: user's own analysis, 2026-11-17
- Confidence: single-source — verify in [[economy/price-sites]] and [[economy/currency-exchange]]
