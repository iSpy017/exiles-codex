# What holds value vs what decays

Scope: rules of thumb for holding or selling, from 0.5 data. Volatile — re-check in 1.0. Detailed timeline → [[league-lifecycle]].

## Holds or gains value (0.5 pattern)
- Claim: Divine Orbs (store of value), endgame crafting currency (Perfect Chaos/Exalted, Fracturing, Annulment), lineage support gems, pinnacle fragments (Simulacrum), week-1 chase league items (Aldur's Legacy, Carved Majesty, Raven-Touched Shard, Perfect Flux, Garukhan's Resolve), meta build-enabling uniques while the build stays meta, and late league low-level spirit gems.
- Version: 0.5
- Volatility: volatile
- Source: user's poe.ninja data — claude/reveal-notes-and-price-findings.md, claude/flipping-strategy.md (2026-11-10), [[league-lifecycle-pricing]] (2026-11-17), tier 4
- Confidence: single-source (user data, two leagues)
- Notes: Lineage supports are drop-only and one-per-character ([[lineage-supports]]) — a structural reason they hold value [INFERRED].

## Loses value
- Claim: Exalted Orbs vs Divines (~93% over 0.5), chase uniques after week 1–2 (Mageblood, Headhunter), most essences and low-tier runes/uncut gems after week 1, items tied to a build after hotfixes nerf it.
- Version: 0.5
- Volatility: volatile
- Source: same user data, tier 4
- Confidence: single-source

## Rules for 1.0 (David's playbook)
- Claim: Sweep profits into Divines every session; sell chase uniques immediately; sell meta-dependent swings when hotfix notes name the build; buy endgame crafting currency and new league chase items early if holding; never hold more than 20% of capital in swings.
- Version: 1.0 (plan)
- Volatility: volatile
- Source: claude/flipping-strategy.md (David's own strategy, 2026-11-10)
