# Gold & its uses

## Properties
- Claim: Gold drops anywhere, can't be traded between players, and is bound to its league. It's spent at NPCs, not used as player-to-player currency.
- Version: 0.5.x
- Volatility: stable
- Source: [PoE2DB Gold](https://poe2db.tw/us/Gold), tier 2; [poe2wiki Gold](https://poe2wiki.net/wiki/Gold), tier 2; retrieved 2026-11-17
- Confidence: confirmed

## Sinks
- Claim: Passive respec (cost per point rises with level — [[respec]]), atlas passive respec, gambling at town vendors, Currency Exchange order fees, async-trade buyer fees, vendor purchases. Some 0.5 sources add Kirac's map vendor (gold per map and per refresh).
- Version: 0.5.x
- Volatility: may-change
- Source: [PoE2DB Gold](https://poe2db.tw/us/Gold), tier 2; [Mobalytics Gold explained](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/gold-explained), tier 3; [poe2wiki Asynchronous trading](https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Asynchronous_trading), tier 2; Grindout / Timesaver gold guides, tier 5; retrieved 2026-11-17
- Confidence: confirmed for respec / gambling / exchange / async fee; Kirac single tier 5
- Notes: Patch 0.1.0e cut respec gold costs (≈40–50% less between start and mid-endgame) ([DSOGaming copy of 0.1.0e notes](https://www.dsogaming.com/?p=185030), tier 5). Big tree changes at 90+ reportedly cost 100k+ gold (Grindout, tier 5 — unverified).

## 1.0: gold buys Conquerors troops
- Claim: In 1.0 the Conquerors league recruits troops with gold earned in the campaign (23 battles), so campaign gold has a new use.
- Version: 1.0 (announced)
- Volatility: may-change
- Source: David's ExileCon reveal notes digest (claude/reveal-notes-and-price-findings.md), secondhand creator summary, tier 5; retrieved 2026-11-17
- Confidence: single-source — verify with 1.0 patch notes
- Notes: Skip route vs gold: skipping zones also skips gold drops [INFERRED]. Keep 2–3× a day's exchange fees in gold for flipping (claude/flipping-strategy.md). → endgame/conquerors-league.

## Getting gold
- Claim: Sell items to vendors; gold piles drop from monsters. Patch 0.2.0g made gold less likely from high-item-rarity monsters but doubled "rare gold" piles so average gold stays similar.
- Version: 0.2.0g+
- Volatility: may-change
- Source: [Vulkk 0.2.0g overview](https://vulkk.com/2025/05/01/path-of-exile-2-update-0-2-0g-changes-overview/), tier 3; [Mobalytics Gold explained](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/gold-explained), tier 3; retrieved 2026-11-17
- Confidence: confirmed
- Notes: Gold-focused farms (Castaway unique map with large gold bonus — [PoE2DB Maps](https://poe2db.tw/Maps), tier 2; Omen of Bartering) → strategy/farm-ranking.
