- Claim: Corpus statements: Atziri's Temple unique boss drops additional Soul Cores ([Atziris Temple](https://poe2db.tw/us/Atziris_Temple)); the Trialmaster in the Trial of Chaos occasionally offers Soul Cores ([The Trial of Chaos](https://poe2db.tw/us/The_Trial_of_Chaos)); a 0.1.1e note says Soul Cores no longer drop outside The Temple of Chaos (old, may be outdated); Thesis cores can sometimes be obtained by modifying a soul core with a Core Destabiliser (drop level 1, 250 gold; modifies a soul core unpredictably with a chance to destroy it, [Core Destabiliser](https://poe2db.tw/us/Core_Destabiliser)).
