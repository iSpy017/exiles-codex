# Interludes (0.5) — removed in 1.0

## Status in 1.0: removed
- Claim: The three Interludes (temporary side stories between Act 4 and the endgame) are removed in 1.0, replaced by Acts 5 and 6. GGG says their areas, bosses and other elements will be reused in other forms.
- Version: 1.0 (announced)
- Volatility: may-change until patch notes
- Source: [PCGamesN interview with Jonathan Rogers](https://www.pcgamesn.com/path-of-exile-2/campaign-final-acts-interview-gamescom), tier 3 (developer quote); [Niche Gamer — Third Edict announcement](https://nichegamer.com/path-of-exile-2-the-third-edict-adds-act-4-and-other-changes) (GGG said the Interludes go away when Acts 5–6 arrive), tier 3; retrieved 2026-11-17
- Confidence: confirmed (two sources quoting GGG)
- Notes: Status → retired at the 1.0 refresh. Rewards below (8 points, 40 spirit, 5% life) must come from somewhere else in 1.0 if totals stay — check Acts 5–6.

## What they were (0.5 reference)
- Claim: Three chains at area level 54–56, each with its own town (The Refuge, The Khari Bazaar, The Glade): Interlude 1 "The Stolen Barya" (Khari: Khari Crossing, Pools of Khatal, Sel Khari Sanctuary, Galai Gates, Qimah, Qimah Reservoir), Interlude 2 "Doryani's Contingency" (Ashen Forest, Kriar Village, Glacial Tarn, Howling Caves, Kriar Peaks, Etched Ravine, Cuachic Vault), Interlude 3 "The Curse of Holten" (Scorched Farmlands, Stones of Serle, Blackwood, Holten, Wolvenhold, Holten Estate).
- Version: 0.5.5.9
- Volatility: retired in 1.0
- Source: [RePoE world_areas.json](https://github.com/repoe-fork/poe2) (P1_/P2_/P3_ ids, act 6 tag), tier 2; [Mobalytics 0.5 campaign checklist](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/0-5-campaign-checklist-stats-rewards-skillpoints-and-more) (chain names), tier 3; retrieved 2026-11-17
- Confidence: confirmed
- Notes: Assigning zones to chains by id prefix is [INFERRED] (P2 = Khari zones, P3 = Kriar zones, P1 = Holten zones).
