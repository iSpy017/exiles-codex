# Quest gem & item rewards (0.5)

Status: done for 0.5 Acts 1–4 + Interludes (item/gem rewards per quest). Some quests show no reward text on PoE2DB. 1.0 will change this (Acts 5–6, Interludes removed). Permanent stat rewards → [[permanent-rewards]].

Main source for the per-act tables: [PoE2DB Quest](https://poe2db.tw/us/Quest), tier 2, retrieved 2026-11-23. The page carries no game version for quest data; its newest league banner is Runes of Aldur 0.5 → treated as 0.5.x. Read through a fetch summary (page is 160k chars), so names and amounts are single-source. Verify anything you plan a route around.

## Act 1 (0.5)
- Claim: Reaching Clearfell → uncut skill L1. Treacherous Ground → uncut support (ilvl 4) + 100 gold. Secrets in the Dark → Runed Spikes. The Lost Lute → Book of Specialisation + 2 passive points + 2 respec points. Sorrow Among Stones → uncut support (ilvl 8). Ominous Altars → choice of Ruby / Sapphire / Topaz Charm (ilvl 11). The Trail of Corruption → uncut skill L5 (ilvl 15). The Mad Wolf of Ogham → uncut support (ilvl 15). Cracks in the Earth → uncut support (ilvl 4) + 100 gold. Blank Runes → choice of Lesser Desert / Glacial / Storm / Iron Rune. The Runeseeker → Verisium (0.5 league item).
- Version: 0.5.x · Volatility: may-change
- Source: PoE2DB Quest (above), tier 2; earlier Mobalytics normal-campaign loot page, tier 3 (boss drops: Brambleghast skill L2, Rust King skill L3, Lachlann skill L3, King in the Mists spirit L4 + 30 spirit)
- Confidence: single-source per item; Act 1 uncut-gem pattern agrees between PoE2DB and Mobalytics (confirmed)
- Notes: the L5 skill from The Trail of Corruption is the Act 1 high point for gems.

Tradition's Toll → Book of Specialisation + 2 passive points + 2 respec points. Ascent to Power → Urn Relic (ilvl 22) + Balbala's Barya.

## Act 3 (0.5)
- Claim: Legacy of the Vaal → Waystone (Tier 1). Unnamed Act 3 entry → choice of Rawhide or Linen Belt (ilvl 39). The Slithering Dead → choice of Venom Draught (Stone / Veil / Clarity) + Artificer's Orb. Treasures of Utzaal → uncut skill L11 (ilvl 41) + 1,000 gold + Artificer's Orb. Tribal Vengeance → choice of Thawing / Staunching / Antidote / Dousing / Grounding Charm (ilvl 38). The Trials of Chaos → Chimeral Inscribed Ultimatum.
- Version: 0.5.x · Volatility: may-change
- Confidence: single-source
- Notes: Chimeral Inscribed Ultimatum = first Trial of Chaos key (ascendancy route). Waystone T1 from Legacy of the Vaal is a free first map.

## Act 4 (0.5)
- Claim: The Search → Boat Charter. The Blind Beast → Sulphite Potion + uncut skill L12 (ilvl 48). Shrike Island → choice of Ruby / Sapphire / Topaz / Amethyst Ring (ilvl 45) + uncut skill L12. Whakapanu Island → uncut skill L12 (two listed). Abandoned Prison → choice of Amber / Jade / Lapis Amulet (ilvl 45) + uncut skill L12. Tribal Medicine → choice of Kaom's Lesson / Rakiata's Lesson. Dark Mists → Verisium Spikes, and a second entry with Mist-shrouded Tome + 7,500 gold + uncut skill L13 (ilvl 55). The Runeseeker → 300 Verisium. Trial of the Ancestors → Tattoo of Hinekora. Siren Pearl → Pearlescent Amulet (ilvl 46). Siege of Oriath → Book of Specialisation. Greater Blank Runes → choice of Greater Desert / Glacial / Storm / Iron / Body / Mind Rune. Tawhoa's Test → Dexterity or Lightning Tattoo of Tawhoa. Tasalio's Test → Intelligence or Cold Tattoo of Tasalio. Ngamahu's Test → Strength or Fire Tattoo of Ngamahu.
- Version: 0.5.x · Volatility: may-change
- Confidence: single-source
- Notes: Act 4 is the richest act for free gear (ring + amulet choices) and gives several L12 skill gems — useful for skill swaps. Act 4 trial count drops in 1.0 ([[dev-statements-1.0-balance]]).

- Version: 0.4 (Guythatdies sheet) / 0.5 (Mobalytics sheet)
- Volatility: may-change (league mechanic changes every league)
- Source: [Mobalytics league mechanic campaign reward cheat sheet](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/league-mechanic-campaign-reward-cheat-sheet), tier 3; [Mobalytics 0.4 cheat sheet](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/profile/guythatdies/guides/0-4-league-mechanic-reward-cheat-sheet), tier 3; retrieved 2026-11-17
- Confidence: single-source per item
- Notes: save early exalts/regals/alchs for the level-16 and 28/33 weapon upgrades → [[levelling-gear]]. 1.0 Conquerors has 20+ campaign missions with their own rewards → [[strategy/official-1.0-reveal]].

## Uncut gem level by drop level
- Claim: Uncut gem level scales with drop level, e.g. drop level 1 → skill L1; 15 → skill L5, support L1; 16 → support L2; 33 → skill L9, support L3; 45 → skill L11, support L4; 55 → skill L13, support L5; 62+ → skill L14–15 and up.
- Version: 0.5.x · Volatility: may-change
- Source: [poe2wiki Uncut Gem](https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Uncut_Gem), tier 2, retrieved 2026-11-17
- Confidence: confirmed (PoE2DB quest ilvls fit: ilvl 15 → skill L5, ilvl 23 → L7, ilvl 41 → L11, ilvl 48 → L12, ilvl 55 → L13)
- Notes: support L2 needs drop level 16 (Act 2 start), L3 needs 33 (Act 3 start).
