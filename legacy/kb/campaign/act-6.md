# Act 6 (1.0) — pre-launch notes

Status: partial — announcement-level only.

## What's announced
- Claim: Act 6 is the final act of the six-act 1.0 campaign. In May 2026 GGG said most Act 5 environment work was done and a little was left for Act 6. GGG's director was "coy" about whether the finale is exactly one act ("more than one act").
- Version: 1.0 (announced)
- Volatility: may-change
- Source: [FRVR interview report](https://frvr.com/blog/news/path-of-exile-2-lead-confirms-1-0-wont-include-all-12-classes/), tier 3; [PCGamesN interview](https://www.pcgamesn.com/path-of-exile-2/campaign-final-acts-interview-gamescom), tier 3; [Maketecheasier — Steam announcement](https://maketecheasier.com/?p=863668), tier 3; retrieved 2026-11-17
- Confidence: confirmed that Acts 5 and 6 ship in 1.0 (multiple sources, incl. Steam announcement); content unknown

## Official counts (Acts 5 + 6 combined)
- Claim: 24 distinct areas, 18 new multi-phase unique bosses and 20+ Champion Rare encounters across Acts 5 and 6. The story ends the Twilight Order and the Beast "once and for all".
- Version: 1.0 (announced)
- Volatility: may-change
- Source: [PoE2 Full release page](https://pathofexile2.com/fullrelease), tier 1, retrieved 2026-11-23
- Confidence: confirmed (tier 1)
- Notes: details in [[campaign/act-5]].
