# Weapon-Set Passive Points

Version: 0.5.x (researched 2026-11-17). Re-check at 1.0. Full mechanics in [[mechanics/weapon-swap]] — this doc is the class-tree view.

## Summary
- Claim: Up to 24 weapon-set points from the campaign (Books of Specialisation give 2 each and also 2 normal points; bonus objectives), +1 from Olroth's Boon. They let that many of your normal points be allocated differently per weapon set. Keystones and jewel sockets can't be set-specific. Shapeshift skills get a third set.
- Version: 0.5.x
- Volatility: may-change — needs-1.0-check (campaign rewards change)
- Source: [poe2wiki Passive Skill Points](https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Passive_Skill_Points), [poe2wiki Weapon set](https://poe2wiki.net/wiki/Weapon_set), tier 2; [Mobalytics Dual Specialisation](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/dual-specialisation), tier 3; retrieved 2026-11-17
- Confidence: confirmed

## Witchhunter: Weapon Master
- Claim: The Witchhunter ascendancy notable Weapon Master turns up to 100 normal points into weapon-set points (was 20 before 0.1.0). It's the only node in game data that grants weapon-set points.
- Version: 0.5.5.9
- Source: [poe2wiki Weapon Master](https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Weapon_Master), tier 2; RePoE Default.json (weapon_set_points = 100), tier 2; retrieved 2026-11-17
- Confidence: confirmed
- Notes: core of the char-2 Frostbolt loop (set I snapshotted damage, set II defences) — poe2-1.0-launch-brief.md.
