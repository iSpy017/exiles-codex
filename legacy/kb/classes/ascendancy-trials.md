# Ascendancy Trials & Points

Version: 0.5.x (researched 2026-11-17). Re-check at 1.0 — the campaign changes and a third trial was planned.

## 8 points, 2 per trial completion
- Claim: Each Ascension Trial completion that meets a difficulty step grants 2 ascendancy points, to a max of 8. Your first completion unlocks your ascendancy choice. Steps can be earned in either trial, the same trial can be repeated for all 8, and one run can grant several steps if it satisfies them.
- Version: 0.5.x
- Volatility: may-change
- Source: [poe2wiki Ascension trials](https://poe2wiki.net/wiki/Ascension_trials), [poe2wiki Ascendancy classes](https://poe2wiki.net/wiki/Ascendancy_classes), tier 2; [Maxroll Trials of Ascendancy](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/getting-started/trials-of-ascendancy), tier 3; retrieved 2026-11-17
- Confidence: confirmed

## The two trials and their keys
- Trial of the Sekhemas (Act 2 desert): needs a Djinn Barya. Quest key Balbala's Barya drops from Balbala, the Traitor (Traitor's Passage, Act 2).
- Trial of Chaos (Act 3): needs an Inscribed Ultimatum. Quest key from Xyclucian, the Chimera (Chimeral Wetlands, Act 3).
- Later keys drop randomly from monsters; their area level and floor/round count decide what you get. Keys now show whether they'll award points.
- Source: [Mobalytics How to Ascend](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/how-to-ascend), tier 3; [poe2wiki Ascendancy classes](https://poe2wiki.net/wiki/Ascendancy_classes), tier 2; retrieved 2026-11-17
- Confidence: confirmed
- Notes: Mobalytics says a failed first Sekhemas attempt keeps the Barya; a failed Inscribed Ultimatum is consumed (Gfinity, tier 5). Trial gameplay → [[endgame/sekhemas]], [[endgame/trial-of-chaos]].

## Changing ascendancy
- Claim: To switch ascendancy you must refund all ascendancy points, then complete a trial at least as hard as the highest you've done.
- Version: 0.5.x
- Source: [poe2wiki Ascension Trial](https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Ascension_Trial), tier 2; [Timesaver ascendancy respec](https://timesaver.gg/tools/poe-2/ascendancy/respec), tier 3; retrieved 2026-11-17
- Confidence: confirmed
- Cost → [[classes/respec]].
