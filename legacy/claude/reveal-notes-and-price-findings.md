# 1.0 Reveal Notes Digest + 0.5 Price Findings (10 Nov 2026)

Source: David's ExileCon reveal notes PDF (secondhand creator summary, not patch notes) and poe.ninja pulled 10 Nov 2026. Dashboard: https://claude.ai/artifact/BKwLBrYZUhPz8k9abReiPA. Raw data in claude/data/.

## New info from the reveal notes (beyond the launch brief)
- Knight: Squire takes gear incl. uniques and skills; War Banners; mounted charge that pushes and can stun.
- Infernalist: new Demon Magus companion that casts given spells; Infernal Hounds significantly buffed.
- Blood Mage: buffed; Exsanguinate added ("looks like a new meta skill"); Corrupted Blood reworked (cracks life, double damage on that portion until healed).
- Mercenary: reload buffed, reload while using other skills.
- Ailments harder to apply but stronger; new Necrotic poison; all chaos DoTs apply Decay. Attack times all scale with attack speed.
- Passive tree: jewel sockets moved into clusters, 18 -> 60 jewel sockets. 5 jewel mods gone. Warrior side buffed.
- Support gems: new system makes supports apply to spell or attack based on the skill.
- Charms: up to 6.
- Atlas: ~70 nodes, ~5x value per node; 25 new maps; each mechanic's first map available in its zone without pathing.
- Conquerors: troops recruited with gold from the campaign (23 battles), castle bosses; endgame stronghold + faction; PvP between factions on the atlas (also in SSF); Commanders run chosen maps (pick waystone difficulty, tablets for chosen mechanics), work offline, travel time by distance/difficulty.
- New currencies: Crimson Iron (Reforging bench: chosen mods replaced with random fractured mods); Blood Veils (replace a chosen mod with a specific mod; class/skill variants).
- Death summary screen confirmed. Pause rework. Arcane Archer ascendancy hoped for 1.0. No Trial of the Ancestors timeline.

## Price findings (0.5, in Divines unless stated)
- Exalted inflation: 1 Divine = 38 ex (day 1) -> 61 (day 3) -> 406 (day 30) -> 549 (now) in Runes of Aldur. Store wealth in Divines.
- Chase uniques peak week 1–2 then fall (Forbidden Rites: Mageblood ~400 div day 3 -> 125; Headhunter 264 div day 7 -> 12.6). Sell immediately.
- Build-enablers climb while the build stays meta (Temporalis, Runeseeker's Call).
- Endgame crafting currency and lineage supports climb 5–20x in Divines over a league.
- Most essences and low-tier runes/uncut gems fade after week 1.
- Caveats: Forbidden Rites adds unique supply via Ritual tribute; late-league "exactly 1 Divine" prices are often stale placeholders.
