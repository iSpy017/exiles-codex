# PoE2 1.0 Launch Brief (researched 10 Nov 2026)

Status: pre-patch-notes. Everything marked 0.5 is the Early Access state and may change in 1.0.

## Launch
- 1.0 full release + free-to-play: 11 Dec 2026 (PST). Launch hour not announced yet.
- Platforms: PC (Steam, Epic, standalone), PS5, Xbox Series X|S. Mac support at 1.0.
- Early Access characters move to "Early Access Standard": separate league, separate economy, no new characters there. 1.0 = full restart.
- Monetisation: cosmetics + stash tabs only.
- Scale: 10 classes, 26 ascendancies, 320+ skills, 400+ uniques, 150+ endgame maps. Acts 5–6 add 24 areas, 18 bosses.

## New classes
### Shadow (daggers, traps, stealth)
- Stealth ambushes; kills reset the Stealth cooldown.
- Traps placed in Stealth detonate in placement order on exit; 8 trap types.
- Blade Vortex pulls embedded blades out of enemies and keeps their effects (chaos / lightning / poison).
- Shades copy your attacks. Darkness resource powers strikes out of Stealth. Eclipse makes every attack count as a stealth attack.
- Ascendancies: Assassin, Infiltrator (node lists not public).
- Semi-AFK fit: poor (timing-heavy).

### Duelist (swords, stances)
- Ready / Sand / Blood stances. Skills include Cyclone and Hemorrhage. Swords: two-hand, dual-wield or with shield.
- Knight: Squire companion (equippable, uses any melee gem), War Banners, Mounted Charge.
- Master-at-Arms: Steam post says 1.0, press text says later. Uncertain for day one.

## Reworks of existing classes
- Infernalist: demon minion casts any spell you give it, up to 3 Infernal Hounds, Covenant skills. Strong semi-AFK candidate.
- Acolyte of Chayula: companion uses any Quarterstaff skill, plus soul clone.
- Blood Mage: new skills and curses, Corrupted Blood rework.
- Mercenary: background reloading of other ammo types.

## League: Conquerors of Wraeclast
- Recruit troops under Varek; 70+ campaign points of interest, 23 campaign battles.
- Endgame: build a stronghold, pick a faction (Karui / Ezomyte / Marraketh).
- Factions fight over 100 castles on the Atlas. Controlled territory = more item rarity + special Hunting Grounds + travel from the atlas.
- Up to 3 commanders run Atlas missions in real time and collect rewards. They keep the gold they find and return worthwhile items. Loot value vs manual mapping unknown.
- Gold pays for castle upgrades and converts into atlas progress.
- Castle treasury rooms drop the new Reforging material.

## Endgame / systems
- Atlas tree cut from 300+ nodes to about 70. Points come from objectives, bosses and special maps; about 50 by the Arbiter of Divinity.
- Passive tree: hundreds of new stats; all jewel sockets now inside clusters.
- Supports: big rebalance. All elemental penetration gems merged into one; Brutality has a smaller chaos penalty; Chain has no damage penalty on the initial hit.
- Items: 200+ new bases, 40+ new uniques, 70+ reworked uniques.
- Performance: up to 25% more CPU performance.

## Crafting
### 0.5 rules (verify for 1.0)
- Rare = 3 prefixes / 3 suffixes. Endgame crafting is done on item level 82+ bases.
- Max one crafted mod (Essence / Perfect Essence / Alloy) + one desecrated mod per item.
- Orbs:
  - Exalted: adds a mod.
  - Chaos: swaps one mod.
  - Divine: rerolls values.
  - Annulment: removes one mod.
  - Vaal: corrupts.
  - Transmutation / Augmentation / Regal / Exalted / Chaos come in Lesser / Greater / Perfect tiers.
- Desecration: apply an Abyssal Bone, reveal 3 options at the Well of Souls, pick 1.
- Key omens:
  - Sinistral / Dextral Necromancy: desecration hits prefixes or suffixes only.
  - Light: Annulment removes only the desecrated mod.
  - Abyssal Echoes: rerolls the 3 desecration options.
  - Sinistral / Dextral Exaltation: Exalted adds a prefix or suffix only.
  - Whittling: Chaos removes the mod with the lowest level requirement.
- Fracturing Orb: locks 1 random mod on a rare with 4+ mods.
- Standard craft: Essence on a high-level base → desecrate (with omen) → Exalted to fill (with omens) → Chaos + Whittling to fix → Divine at the end.

### New in 1.0
- Reforging: replaces unwanted mods with permanently fractured mods, including new blood-infused effects. The material comes from castle treasuries. Expect it to be pricey early.

## Economy and early money
- Exalted is the working currency early. 1 Divine ≈ 354 Exalted on 27 Aug (0.5.x), so expect huge swings. Convert to Divines once the farm loop is stable.
- Sell drops: rares with T1 life / resist / damage on good bases. Bulk-sell currency on the Currency Exchange.
- Specialise the atlas on 1–2 mechanics. In 0.5: Expedition = burst income, Abyss = steady, Ritual = Omens hold value.
- New league mechanics pay most early (castles, Hunting Grounds, Reforging material).
- Craft-for-profit loop: strong bases → Perfect Transmutation / Augmentation → Essence + Exalted → Desecrate → sell.

## Reference build for character 2: Immortal Witchhunter Frostbolt Infinite Loop (0.5.5, Lazy Exile)
- Guide: https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/profile/lazy-exile/builds/0-5-5-immortal-witchhunter-frostbolt-infinite-loop
- Snapshots:
  - Budget: https://poe.ninja/poe2/pob/2937a
  - Expensive: https://poe.ninja/poe2/pob/297af
- Mercenary / Witchhunter.
- Loop: start on weapon set II (Mirror of Refraction spawns passively) → cast Frostbolt at the mirror → cast Frost Wall. Never swap back to set I.
- Set I passives = cold / crit / projectile damage, snapshotted via Weapon Master. Set II = cooldown recovery, evasion, deflection.
- Frostbolt supports: Breachlord's Amalgam, Considered Casting, Zenith II, Deliberation, Cold Penetration.
- Frost Wall supports: Ahn's Citadel, Kaom's Madness, Glacier, Spell Cascade, Cold Mastery.
- Other skills only trigger Gem Enthusiast: Shockwave Totem, Seismic Cry, Infernal Cry, Escape Shot, Blasphemy (+ Temporal Chains).
- Defence: Sorcery Ward + evasion; Mind Over Matter on the tree.
- Minimum stats: >85% increased cooldown recovery, >60% projectile speed, 2,000 life, 1,000 mana, 8,000 Sorcery Ward, capped deflection, 10,000 evasion.
- Key jewel: From Nothing (Iron Reflexes → Gem Enthusiast, General's Bindings, Authority). Expensive setup is hundreds of Divines (one user reported about 600).
- Strengths: near-immortal, 0-button, AFK Simulacrum, clears all content including pinnacles.
- Weakness: SLOW MAPPER. Not a league starter; people level another build first (e.g. Tactician) and swap at 75–80.
- 1.0 risk: uniques, lineage supports and Mercenary are all being changed. Verify after patch notes.
