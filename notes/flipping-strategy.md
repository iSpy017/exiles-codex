# Day-3+ Flipping Strategy (project copy)

Live doc: https://claude.ai/artifact/4QWdyXdUWboAGq3P4vZUTW. Written 10 Nov 2026, pre-patch-notes, from 0.5 behaviour.

## Bottom line
Start on day 3, once the farmer runs on its own and you hold ~1 Divine of working capital. Flip only items with volume; go only if the margin clears the gold fee by 10% or more.

## Tools
- Built-in price check (Shift + Alt + Click, since 0.5.0): quick reads.
- Exiled Exchange 2: install only from github.com/Kvan7/Exiled-Exchange-2 or kvan7.github.io. Game must be in English.
- pathofexile.com/trade2: search the 2–3 mods that carry the value, read the lowest 5–10 live listings.
- poe.ninja: spread, volume, 7-day trend (exchange data up to 1 hour stale).

## Method 1: Currency Exchange arbitrage
Buy order just above best buy, sell order just below best sell, on liquid items (Divine, Exalted, Chaos, Annulment, popular essences/omens). Spread minus gold fee must be >= 10% of capital tied up. Check the Divine -> Exalted -> Chaos -> Divine loop. Gold can't be traded: keep 2–3x a day's fees in gold.

## Method 2: bulk stackables
Buy singles, sell stacks 5–10% over singles (10–20 per currency listing, 5 per fragment). 0.5 (Exalted, day 3 -> day 30 -> now): Perfect Chaos 109 -> 1560 -> 3320; Perfect Exalted 124 -> 799 -> 1540; Fracturing 246 -> 3250 -> 3660; Annulment 13 -> 234 -> 237; Simulacrum 345 -> 1100 -> 1550. Most greater essences fade after week 1: sell them, don't stockpile.

## Method 3: sniping
5–10 saved searches on items you know (meta build-enablers, high jewels, ilvl 82+ T1 bases). Buy listings 30%+ under the next 5, check for traps (wrong base, corrupted, worse rolls, offline seller), relist just under market via Merchant's Tab. Unsold 24 h: -10%. 48 h: sell at market.

## Method 4: swing trades on new 1.0 items
0.5 chase items were cheapest in week 1 and rose 5–20x in Divines (Garukhan's Resolve, Aldur's Legacy, Carved Majesty, Raven-Touched Shard, Perfect Flux). 1.0 candidates [INFERRED]: Blood Veils (meta variants), Crimson Iron, jewels (18 -> 60 sockets), tablets (commanders), gold. Max 20% of capital in swings; set the exit before buying.

## Capital rules
Farmer upgrades come off the top. Then: arbitrage 50%, bulk 20%, swings <= 20%, snipe float 10%. Minimum margin: 10% arbitrage, 20% snipes, 2x swings. Sweep profit to Divines every session (Exalted lost ~93% vs Divine over 0.5). 30–45 min of flipping a day in launch week.

## Risks and stop rules
Skip items under ~1 Divine/day volume (1-Divine placeholder prices). Watch for bait listings. Sell meta-dependent swings when hotfix notes name the build. Three losing flips in a row on one item = stop trading it for the day.
