# Starter Decision Sheet (fill on patch-notes day)

Status: template, updated 17 Nov 2026 (was 10 Nov), pre-1.0 patch notes. Score each 1-5, multiply by weight, sum. Highest total wins, unless it fails a gate.

Order of decisions (17 Nov): pick the farm first (most profitable content, see Farm shortlist below), then score starters against that farm. Framework source: kb/strategy/league-start-framework.md.

## Gates (fail any = out)
1. Can reach real layered defences by red maps (David: glass cannon OK early, not once maps scale).
2. Core skill/ascendancy not named in a nerf in the 1.0 notes.
3. Campaign playable on the same skill (one character, no reroll; respec at early maps is fine).
4. 5-divine T15 test (added 17 Nov): can farm T15s in the chosen farm content with ≤5 div of gear. List the first 3–5 upgrades and their expected cost before launch.

## Weights (re-weighted 17 Nov for farm-first + super-sweat pacing)
| Criterion | Weight | What a 5 looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clear speed | 25% | Screen-wide clear, fast movement, no setup per pack |
| Farm fit (added 17 Nov) | 20% | Meets the chosen farm's needs (e.g. Ritual: AoE + defence vs spawns around you in a tight space) |
| Defence ceiling by red maps | 15% | Layered (max res + armour/evasion/ES + recovery); survives juiced one-shots |
| Campaign speed | 15% | Strong from Act 1, no dead zone, fast on a skip route |
| Low-thinking play | 10% | 1-2 buttons, walk and kill |
| Patch-note risk | 10% | Buffed or untouched in 1.0 notes; no lineage/unique dependency |
| Cost to get online | 5% | Works on rares + 1-2 cheap uniques by early maps (main cost check is now gate 4) |

Changes from 10 Nov: clear 30→25, defence 20→15 (gate 1 still enforces the floor), cost 15→5 (moved into gate 4), campaign smoothness → campaign speed, farm fit added at 20.
