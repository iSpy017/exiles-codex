## What they are
@ v=0.5.x vol=may-change conf=confirmed date=2026-11-17
Lineage supports are the "unique items" of support gems. They can't be engraved from Uncut Support Gems — they only drop (global random drops, or rewards/drops from specific encounters and bosses). A character can use only one copy of each lineage support (Solus Ipse unique bypasses this).

- conf: confirmed (wiki, multiple pages)
- notes: e.g. Eonyr's Thunder requires level 65 ([poe2wiki Lineage (gem tag)](https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Lineage_(gem_tag))).
