# Lineage Support Gems

Version: game data 0.5.5.9 + 0.5.x (researched 2026-11-17). Re-check at 1.0.

## What they are
- Claim: Lineage supports are the "unique items" of support gems. They can't be engraved from Uncut Support Gems — they only drop (global random drops, or rewards/drops from specific encounters and bosses). A character can use only one copy of each lineage support (Solus Ipse unique bypasses this).
- Version: 0.5.x
- Volatility: may-change
- Source: [poe2wiki Lineage](https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Lineage), [poe2wiki Abyss lineage support](https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Abyss_lineage_support), [poe2wiki Spirit Gem](https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Spirit_Gem), tier 2; retrieved 2026-11-17
- Confidence: confirmed (wiki, multiple pages)
- Notes: e.g. Eonyr's Thunder requires level 65 ([poe2wiki Lineage (gem tag)](https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Lineage_(gem_tag))).
