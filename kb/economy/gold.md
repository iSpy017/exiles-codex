## Properties
@ v=0.5.x vol=stable conf=confirmed date=2026-11-17
Gold drops anywhere, can't be traded between players, and is bound to its league. It's spent at NPCs, not used as player-to-player currency.

- src: poe2db:Gold, tier 2; poe2wiki Gold [S621], tier 2; retrieved 2026-11-17

## Sinks
@ v=0.5.x vol=may-change conf=confirmed date=2026-11-17
Passive respec (cost per point rises with level — [[respec]]), atlas passive respec, gambling at town vendors, Currency Exchange order fees, async-trade buyer fees, vendor purchases. Some 0.5 sources add Kirac's map vendor (gold per map and per refresh).
- src: poe2db:Gold, tier 2; Mobalytics Gold explained [S069], tier 3; poe2wiki Asynchronous trading [S667], tier 2; Grindout / Timesaver gold guides, tier 5; retrieved 2026-11-17
- conf: confirmed for respec / gambling / exchange / async fee; Kirac single tier 5
- notes: Patch 0.1.0e cut respec gold costs (≈40–50% less between start and mid-endgame) ([DSOGaming copy of 0.1.0e notes](https://www.dsogaming.com/?p=185030), tier 5). Big tree changes at 90+ reportedly cost 100k+ gold (Grindout, tier 5 — unverified).

@ v=1.0 vol=may-change conf=single-source date=2026-11-17
In 1.0 the Conquerors league recruits troops with gold earned in the campaign (23 battles), so campaign gold has a new use.

## Getting gold
@ v=0.5.x vol=may-change conf=confirmed date=2026-11-17
Sell items to vendors; gold piles drop from monsters. Patch 0.2.0g made gold less likely from high-item-rarity monsters but doubled "rare gold" piles so average gold stays similar.
- src: Vulkk 0.2.0g overview [S648], tier 3; Mobalytics Gold explained [S069], tier 3; retrieved 2026-11-17
- v: 0.2.0g+
- notes: Gold-focused farms (Castaway unique map with large gold bonus — [PoE2DB Maps](https://poe2db.tw/Maps), tier 2; Omen of Bartering) → strategy/farm-ranking.
