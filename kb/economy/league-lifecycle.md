## Day 1–3
@ v=0.5 vol=volatile conf=single-source date=2026-11-10
Chase uniques and new league items start high-volume and cheap relative to later (in Divines) for build-enablers, but chase uniques are at their peak in week 1–2 (Forbidden Rites: Mageblood ~400 div on day 3 → 125 later; Headhunter 264 div day 7 → 12.6). Endgame crafting currency is cheapest early: Perfect Chaos 109 ex (day 3) → 1,560 (day 30) → 3,320; Perfect Exalted 124 → 799 → 1,540; Fracturing Orb 246 → 3,250 → 3,660; Annulment 13 → 234 → 237; Simulacrum 345 → 1,100 → 1,550.
- src: user's poe.ninja data (claude/flipping-strategy.md, claude/reveal-notes-and-price-findings.md, 2026-11-10), tier 4
- v: 0.5 (Runes of Aldur / Forbidden Rites)
- conf: single-source (user data)
- notes: Those currency figures are in Exalts, so part of the rise is Exalt inflation — compare in Divines. Still, the 0.5 notes say endgame crafting currency and lineage supports climb 5–20× in Divines over a league.

## Mid → late league
@ v=0.5 vol=volatile conf=single-source date=2026-11-17
Build-enablers climb while the build stays meta (Temporalis, Runeseeker's Call). Chase uniques keep falling. 0.5 week-1 chase items rose 5–20× in Divines by late league (Garukhan's Resolve, Aldur's Legacy, Carved Majesty, Raven-Touched Shard, Perfect Flux). Late league: low-level spirit gems spike (alt-starters), basic quality currency trades above 1 ex each.

- notes: Forbidden Rites adds unique supply via Ritual tribute — a caveat on unique decay rates. Late-league "exactly 1 div" listings are often stale placeholders.
