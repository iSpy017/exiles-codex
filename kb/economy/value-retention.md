## Holds or gains value (0.5 pattern)
@ v=0.5 vol=volatile conf=single-source date=2026-11-17
Divine Orbs (store of value), endgame crafting currency (Perfect Chaos/Exalted, Fracturing, Annulment), lineage support gems, pinnacle fragments (Simulacrum), week-1 chase league items (Aldur's Legacy, Carved Majesty, Raven-Touched Shard, Perfect Flux, Garukhan's Resolve), meta build-enabling uniques while the build stays meta, and late league low-level spirit gems.

## Loses value
@ v=0.5 vol=volatile conf=single-source date=2026-11-17 fill=date
Exalted Orbs vs Divines (~93% over 0.5), chase uniques after week 1–2 (Mageblood, Headhunter), most essences and low-tier runes/uncut gems after week 1, items tied to a build after hotfixes nerf it.
