## Summary
@ v=0.5.x vol=may-change conf=confirmed date=2026-11-17
Up to 24 weapon-set points from the campaign (Books of Specialisation give 2 each and also 2 normal points; bonus objectives), +1 from Olroth's Boon. They let that many of your normal points be allocated differently per weapon set. Keystones and jewel sockets can't be set-specific. Shapeshift skills get a third set.

## Witchhunter: Weapon Master
@ v=0.5.5.9 vol=? conf=confirmed date=2026-11-17 missing=vol
The Witchhunter ascendancy notable Weapon Master turns up to 100 normal points into weapon-set points (was 20 before 0.1.0). It's the only node in game data that grants weapon-set points.
