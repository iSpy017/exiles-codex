# Respec Costs

Version: 0.5.x (researched 2026-11-17; full gold table added batch 17). Re-check at 1.0.

## Where
- Claim: Refund passives at The Hooded One (unlocked in Act 1 via the "Mysterious Shade" quest) or Doryani (Act 3 / hideout, endgame).
- Version: 0.5.x
- Source: [Mobalytics Dual Specialisation](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/dual-specialisation), [Maxroll Passive Tree](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/poe-2-passive-tree-and-dual-specialization), tier 3; [Digital Trends](https://digitaltrends.com/gaming/how-to-respec-path-of-exile-2), tier 5; retrieved 2026-11-17
- Confidence: confirmed

## Attribute node switch
- Claim: Changing which attribute a travel/attribute node gives costs half the gold of refunding one passive, with no need to refund nodes after it.
- Version: 0.5.x
- Source: [poe2wiki Passive Skill Tree](https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Passive_Skill_Tree), tier 2, retrieved 2026-11-17 (earlier "full respec" claim → kb/04-conflicts.md)
- Confidence: single-source; conflict open

## Ascendancy points
- Claim: Refunding ascendancy points costs 5× a normal passive refund (scales with level). To change ascendancy, refund all, then redo a trial at your highest difficulty.
- Version: 0.5.x
- Source: [Timesaver ascendancy respec](https://timesaver.gg/tools/poe-2/ascendancy/respec), tier 5 (boosting/currency site, lead only); [poe2wiki Ascension Trial](https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Ascension_Trial), tier 2; retrieved 2026-11-17
- Confidence: single-source (5×, tier 5); confirmed (trial requirement)

## Weapon-set refunds
- Points allocated in both sets: remove one from each to free one normal point. Source: poe2wiki Passive Skill Tree, tier 2, 2026-11-17. Confidence: confirmed (2 wiki pages).

## Plan impact
- [INFERRED] Gold is also needed for Conquerors troops/castles in 1.0 (launch brief). A full respec at L65–70 is roughly 70–85k gold for ~40 points — budget it before the planned early-maps respec into the farmer. Reason: wiki table × point count.
- Cost is by character level, so respec early (campaign) is cheap; 1.0 level-scaling unchecked.
