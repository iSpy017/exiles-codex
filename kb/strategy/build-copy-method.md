## Pick / learn / copy
@ v=0.5.5 vol=stable conf=single-source date=2026-11-17
Don't copy builds one-for-one; learn why they work. Pick: use videos from the current patch that show hard content (not only T15 mapping), check the skill you'd actually play and your goal. Learn: check legitimacy (who plays it), understand the mechanism (e.g. what actually triggers your procs), circle non-negotiable gear stats, check the poe.ninja jewels/uniques to set a budget, decide the defence layer (life/ES/armour/evasion), price chase uniques. Copy: "close enough is good enough" — hit the required stats, not the exact numbers.

## Concrete rules in it
@ v=0.5.5 vol=may-change conf=single-source date=2026-11-17 fill=v,vol
- Resistances: work out your base resistance without gear (example −40%), add 75, and plan the needed total across all gear before buying so the last slot isn't forced into a mirror-priced piece. This matches the overcap/cap logic in [[mechanics/resistances]].
- Don't start copying a build before about level 90: level-20 gems and enough passive points aren't available earlier, so the copy will fall short.
- Diagnose failures: dying = defensive gap (res, ES/life, armour/evasion, ailments); not killing = damage gap or broken interaction; clunky = mechanical (triggers, speed); worse than the video = currency.
- 60-second gut check: right patch? shows bosses? uses chase uniques?
