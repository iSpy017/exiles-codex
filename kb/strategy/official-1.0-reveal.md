# Official 1.0 reveal page (pre-patch-notes)

Status: done (tier 1, announcement text). Retire or merge once the 1.0 patch notes land (due ~1 week before 11 Dec per Exiled Bench citing Maxroll; not yet out as of 2026-11-23).
Source for every entry unless noted: [Path of Exile 2 — Full release page](https://pathofexile2.com/fullrelease), tier 1, read 2026-11-23 via built-in browser (page needs JavaScript; plain fetch returns nothing).

## Launch basics
- Claim: Full release on 11 Dec 2026. PoE2 becomes free to play that day. Five new Supporter Packs.
- Version: 1.0 (announced) · Volatility: stable · Confidence: confirmed (also Exiled Bench, lfcarry — tier 5)

## Campaign: Acts 5 and 6
- Claim: Acts 5 and 6 complete a six-act campaign. Setting = Oriath, with the Twilight Order and "the Beast" as the story antagonists. Together the two acts have 24 distinct areas (named examples: slums, estates, Temple District, Grand Arena). 18 new multi-phase unique bosses. Over 20 new Champion Rare encounters, each with its own mechanics.
- Version: 1.0 (announced) · Volatility: may-change · Confidence: confirmed (accountshark summary repeats the same counts, tier 5)
- Notes: zone list, area levels and permanent rewards still unknown → [[campaign/act-5]], [[campaign/act-6]].

## New class: Shadow
- Claim: Uses daggers, traps and shades. New mechanic Stealth: monsters can't see you until you're within a short distance. Being in Stealth empowers dagger skills (examples given: poison explosions, shadow clones). Traps are a new weapon type; they're prepared in Stealth and trigger automatically when you strike, exploding in throw order. Each trap interacts with at least three others. Two ascendancies at release; the page names the Assassin.
- Version: 1.0 (announced) · Volatility: may-change · Confidence: confirmed (tier 1)
- Notes: second Shadow ascendancy (Infiltrator per [[classes/index]]) isn't named on this page.

## New class: Duelist
- Claim: Sword user. New mechanic Stances: Ready Stance empowers sword skills. Upgrades: stunning an enemy → Sand Stance (sand disks); parrying → Blood Stance (hits make enemies bleed). Two ascendancies "in 1.0"; the page names the Master-at-Arms.
- Version: 1.0 (announced) · Volatility: may-change · Confidence: confirmed (tier 1)
- Notes: this settles the launch-brief worry that Master-at-Arms might not be there on day one: the page presents it as a 1.0 ascendancy. Second one (Knight) isn't named here. See 04-conflicts.

## Conquerors of Wraeclast: campaign layer
- Claim: You join Varek and his mercenaries. During the campaign: over 70 new points of interest (troop recruitment, unique missions) and over 20 new campaign missions (defend villages, assault forts, break gates). Units have strengths and weaknesses; during a battle you push the enemy back line and kill their strongest fighters.
- Version: 1.0 · Volatility: may-change · Confidence: single-source (tier 1)
- Notes: Maxroll's 1.0 summary (search snippet only, tier 3) says "17 new points of interest" → logged in 04-conflicts. Reveal notes said 23 campaign battles (tier 5) vs "over 20" here: consistent.

## Conquerors: stronghold and factions (endgame)
- Claim: In the endgame you build a stronghold with Varek. Choose one of three factions, each with its own fortress style and unique troops. 20+ buildings and upgrades per faction (examples: barracks, taverns, archery ranges, cultist housing).
- Version: 1.0 · Volatility: may-change · Confidence: confirmed (matches [[dev-statements-1.0-league]])

## Reforging (new crafting material)
- Claim: Conquered castles have treasury rooms with gold, items, and often a new crafting material. It replaces an unwanted modifier with a random **fractured** modifier, which stays locked through later crafting.
- Version: 1.0 · Volatility: may-change · Confidence: confirmed (koni_pu3 notes, Exiled Bench, tier 5; matches dev statements)
- Notes: Maxroll snippet (tier 3, unverified): fractured items can't be Forgemarked further. → [[crafting/league-crafting]] at refresh.

## Blood Lord storyline
- Claim: New endgame storyline about the Sanguar bloodline: stop the awakening of the Blood Lord by sieging the Sanguar stronghold.
- Version: 1.0 · Volatility: may-change · Confidence: single-source (tier 1)

## Commanders ("Automate your Atlas")
- Claim: Real-time system: give a commander troops and Waystones and their army clears maps and collects rewards. Up to three commanders as the stronghold grows. Commanders can explore the Atlas, complete Towers, or hunt specific rewards using Tablets.
- Version: 1.0 · Volatility: may-change · Confidence: confirmed (Maxroll ExileCon recap snippet: commanders unlock near the end of endgame progression, tier 3)
- Notes: big farm-strategy lever for David's AFK-second-character goal → [[strategy/farm-ranking]] once notes land.

## Global faction war
- Claim: Unlocks after finishing the endgame storyline. Your whole faction competes for 100+ castles across the league. Holding another faction's castle gives benefits incl. increased item rarity and special maps with rewarding modifiers. Any faction-held castle can be a launch point for exploring the Atlas.
- Version: 1.0 · Volatility: may-change · Confidence: single-source (tier 1); Maxroll recap: one faction taking over the whole Atlas "shouldn't happen" (tier 3 snippet)

## Items, gems, tree
- Claim: 40+ new uniques; 70+ existing uniques reworked (mods, skills, visuals). New skills for classes that lacked them (examples: molten-forge mace slams, soul extraction for more damage). Support gems massively rebalanced (better recommendations, damage types more adaptable, underperformers buffed). Passive tree: hundreds of new stats; all jewel sockets moved into the clusters.
- Version: 1.0 · Volatility: may-change · Confidence: confirmed (accountshark, tier 5, same figures)
- Notes: jewel-socket move → [[classes/passive-tree]] needs-1.0-check (already flagged).

## Class reworks shown
- Claim: The page shows the Mercenary rework: ammo reloads in the background while you use other ammo types, so switching is faster.
- Version: 1.0 · Volatility: may-change · Confidence: confirmed (GGG press release, see [[builds/starter-archetypes]])
