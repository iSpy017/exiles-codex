## Campaign: Acts 5 and 6
@ v=1.0 vol=may-change conf=confirmed date=2026-11-23 fill=src
Acts 5 and 6 complete a six-act campaign. Setting = Oriath, with the Twilight Order and "the Beast" as the story antagonists. Together the two acts have 24 distinct areas (named examples: slums, estates, Temple District, Grand Arena). 18 new multi-phase unique bosses. Over 20 new Champion Rare encounters, each with its own mechanics.

## New class: Shadow
@ v=1.0 vol=may-change conf=confirmed date=2026-11-23 fill=src
Uses daggers, traps and shades. New mechanic Stealth: monsters can't see you until you're within a short distance. Being in Stealth empowers dagger skills (examples given: poison explosions, shadow clones). Traps are a new weapon type; they're prepared in Stealth and trigger automatically when you strike, exploding in throw order. Each trap interacts with at least three others. Two ascendancies at release; the page names the Assassin.
- v: 1.0 (announced)
- conf: confirmed (tier 1)
- notes: second Shadow ascendancy (Infiltrator per [[classes/index]]) isn't named on this page.


## New class: Duelist
@ v=1.0 vol=may-change conf=confirmed date=2026-11-23 fill=src
Sword user. New mechanic Stances: Ready Stance empowers sword skills. Upgrades: stunning an enemy → Sand Stance (sand disks); parrying → Blood Stance (hits make enemies bleed). Two ascendancies "in 1.0"; the page names the Master-at-Arms, the press release names the Knight.
- v: 1.0 (announced)
- conf: confirmed (tier 1, two GGG texts)
- notes: settles the launch-brief worry that Master-at-Arms might not be there on day one. See 04-conflicts.


## Conquerors of Wraeclast: campaign layer
@ v=1.0 vol=may-change conf=confirmed date=2026-11-23 fill=src
You join Varek and his mercenaries. During the campaign: over 70 new points of interest (troop recruitment, unique missions) and over 20 new campaign missions (defend villages, assault forts, break gates); the press release says 23 campaign battles. Units have strengths and weaknesses; during a battle you push the enemy back line and kill their strongest fighters.

## Conquerors: stronghold and factions (endgame)
@ v=1.0 vol=may-change conf=confirmed date=2026-11-23 fill=src
In the endgame you build a stronghold with Varek. Choose one of three factions, each with its own fortress style and unique troops. 20+ buildings and upgrades per faction (examples: barracks, taverns, archery ranges, cultist housing).

- conf: confirmed (matches [[dev-statements-1.0-league]])

## Reforging (new crafting material)
@ v=1.0 vol=may-change conf=confirmed date=2026-11-23 fill=src
Conquered castles have treasury rooms with gold, items, and often a new crafting material. It replaces an unwanted modifier with a random **fractured** modifier, which stays locked through later crafting.
- conf: confirmed (koni_pu3 notes, Exiled Bench, tier 5; matches dev statements)
- notes: Maxroll snippet (tier 3, unverified): fractured items can't be Forgemarked further. → [[crafting/league-crafting]] at refresh.

## Commanders ("Automate your Atlas")
@ v=1.0 vol=may-change conf=confirmed date=2026-11-23 fill=src
Real-time system: give a commander troops and Waystones and their army clears maps and collects rewards. Up to three commanders as the stronghold grows. Commanders can explore the Atlas, complete Towers, or hunt specific rewards using Tablets.
- conf: confirmed (Maxroll ExileCon recap snippet: commanders unlock near the end of endgame progression, tier 3)
- notes: big farm-strategy lever for David's AFK-second-character goal → [[strategy/farm-ranking]] once notes land.


## Global faction war
@ v=1.0 vol=may-change conf=single-source date=2026-11-23 fill=src
Unlocks after finishing the endgame storyline. Your whole faction competes for 100+ castles across the league. Holding another faction's castle gives benefits incl. increased item rarity and special maps with rewarding modifiers. Any faction-held castle can be a launch point for exploring the Atlas.

- conf: single-source (tier 1); Maxroll recap: one faction taking over the whole Atlas "shouldn't happen" (tier 3 snippet)
