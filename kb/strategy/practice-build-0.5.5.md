## Why it should carry over best
1. Not on GGG's rework list. Official 1.0 press release names major reworks for Mercenary, Infernalist, Blood Mage and Acolyte of Chayula only ([GGG press release](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/4013917/page/1), tier 1). Deadeye and bows aren't named → [INFERRED] lowest-risk of the decision-sheet candidates. Knight doesn't exist in 0.5.5; Infernalist and Blood Mage are reworked; Grenade Gemling (what you already practised) sits on the reworked Mercenary.
2. Matches your weights. Clear speed 25%, low-thinking 10%: Fubgun describes 1–2 button mapping; can later drop Snipe for one button.
3. The parts that transfer regardless of 1.0 balance [INFERRED]: Acts 1–4 routing and vendor checks, the martial weapon craft loop at 10–11 / 16 / 28 / 33 ([[builds/levelling-gear]]), resist checkpoints, the defence checklist ([[builds/defence-checklist]]), bow base knowledge, and the 5-div T15 test discipline.

## What will NOT carry over — don't over-learn these [INFERRED from 1.0 announcements]
- Passive tree pathing: tree gets a massive overhaul with jewel sockets moved into clusters (tier 1). Learn the *shape* (which notables matter), not the click order.
- Support links: supports are rebalanced (tier 1); Chain loses its first-hit penalty and elemental penetration supports merge (launch brief, secondhand). Expect link changes.
- Freeze/Snipe boss loop: secondhand reveal notes say ailments become harder to apply but stronger → Ice Shot's freeze-then-Snipe timing may change. Lightning Arrow's shock reliance has the same risk.
- Interludes route: removed in 1.0 ([[campaign/interludes]]). Practise Acts 1–4 only.
- Uniques: 70+ reworked; don't plan around a 0.5.5 unique.

## Tweaks to practise it "like 1.0"
- Super-sweat route, Acts 1–4: time each act; note where you lose time.
- Pretend the economy is day 1: no buying from trade during the campaign; craft weapons yourself at the breakpoints.
- Run gate 4 for real: reach T15 and write down the first 3–5 upgrades and what they cost.
- Check gate 1: Deadeye is low on early defence (archetype note in [[builds/starter-archetypes]]); find out at which tier it stops feeling safe.
- Late-league bonus: sell the campaign L8 uncut spirit gem and buy an L10/L12 once level allows ([[strategy/league-lifecycle-pricing]]; L10 needs char 36, L12 needs 46 per [[gems/gem-levels]]). Check the exchange first — prices are from 2026-11-17.

## Runners-up
- Falling Thunder Martial Artist (Fubgun S): Monk ascendancy not on the rework list, but slow early game and not a decision-sheet candidate.
- Grim Pillars Oracle (Fubgun S): Druid not on the rework list; needs a strong PC; not a candidate.
- Grenade Gemling: you already practised it; Mercenary is reworked → lowest carry-over.
