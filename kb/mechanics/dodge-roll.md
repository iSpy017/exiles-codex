# Dodge Roll & Sprint

Version: 0.5.x (researched 2026-11-17). Re-check at 1.0. No dodge-roll keyword in game data; sources are poe2wiki, poe2db community wiki, Mobalytics.

## Basics
- Claim: Every character has a dodge roll on its own keybind (spacebar default), no gem, no cooldown, no cost. It cancels almost any animation except another roll.
- Version: 0.5.x
- Volatility: may-change
- Source: [poe2wiki Dodge roll](https://poe2wiki.net/wiki/Dodge_roll), [poe2wiki Dodged](https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Dodged), tier 2; [PoE2DB Dodge Roll](https://poe2db.tw/fr/Dodge_Roll) (community wiki section), tier 2; [Mobalytics Dodge Roll](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/dodge-roll), tier 3; retrieved 2026-11-17
- Confidence: confirmed

## What it avoids
- Claim: The first half of the roll avoids projectiles and non-AoE (melee/strike) attacks. It does NOT avoid AoE or ground effects. PoE2DB's wiki phrases this as "no invulnerability" but the same avoidance window — practical effect is identical.
- Version: 0.5.x
- Volatility: may-change
- Source: [poe2wiki Dodged](https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Dodged), tier 2; [PoE2DB Dodge Roll](https://poe2db.tw/us/Dodge_Roll), tier 2; retrieved 2026-11-17
- Confidence: confirmed

## Speed / distance
- Claim: Not faster than running; roll speed scales with movement speed. Distance roughly 3.7 m.
- Version: 0.5.x
- Volatility: may-change
- Source: [PoE2DB Dodge Roll](https://poe2db.tw/fr/Dodge_Roll), tier 2 (speed); 3.7 m from tier-5 sources only
- Confidence: single-source (speed); [INFERRED] 3.7 m — verify

## Keystones that change it
- Unwavering Stance: can't be light stunned, but no dodge roll or sprint. (game keywords; [poe2wiki Dodge roll](https://poe2wiki.net/wiki/Dodge_roll) also says it doubles stun threshold)
- Bulwark: rolls no longer avoid damage, instead you take 30% less hit damage while rolling. ([poe2wiki Dodge roll](https://poe2wiki.net/wiki/Dodge_roll); [PoE2DB Dodge Roll](https://poe2db.tw/us/Dodge_Roll) for the 30%)
- Version: 0.5.x, confidence confirmed (both named in 2 sources)

## Sprint
- Claim: Hold the roll key after a roll to sprint: 50% increased movement speed, but getting hit can knock you down. Any skill use ends it. A settings toggle can make the key toggle sprint. Pathfinder's Running Assault prevents knockdown. No roll = no sprint (e.g. with Blink active).
- Version: 0.5.x (sprint added 0.3.1)
- Volatility: may-change
- Source: [poe2wiki Sprint](https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Sprint), tier 2; [Mobalytics Sprinting](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/sprinting), tier 3; retrieved 2026-11-17
- Confidence: confirmed (50%, knockdown); single-source (Running Assault)
- Notes: sprint is the skip-route travel tool between packs → [[strategy/skip-route]].
