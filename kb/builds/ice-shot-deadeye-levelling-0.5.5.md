- Day-1 economy: no trade buying; craft weapons yourself.
- Log: level at each boss, tier where Deadeye stops feeling safe (gate 1), first 3–5 T15 upgrades and cost (gate 4).

## 1. Skills and supports
**Levelling setup [ML]**
| Skill | Supports |
|---|---|
| Lightning Arrow | Rapid Attacks I, Elemental Armament I |
| Lightning Rod | Concentrated Area, Elemental Armament I |
- Support priority: Rapid Attacks I → Elemental Armament I → Concentrated Area → second Elemental Armament I.
- Boss fights: move Elemental Armament I from Lightning Arrow onto Lightning Rod. Also do this if strength is too low for both.
- Spirit: Herald of Thunder until Ice Shot unlocks, then Herald of Ice.
- Optional: Pounce (needs a talisman in offhand; drop it when slots run short, mid Act 2–3). Others listed: Barrage, Ice-Tipped Arrows, Frost Bomb.

**Gem pickup order [ML]:** Lightning Rod first, then Frost Bomb — both before Devourer and Cold Witch. Clearfell chest = reliable L1 skill gem. Rapid Attacks I after Devourer. Elemental Armament I after Hut Witch. [INFERRED] Cold Witch = Beira, Hut Witch = Areagne (names match the Act 1 boss list in [[campaign/act-1]]).
**Level 24:** mini skill swap. Hoard uncut L6/L7 gems and supports; use none before it ([ML]; the swap itself isn't described).
**Level 31:** Ice Shot unlocks [ML]. Support L2 needs drop level 16, L3 needs 33 ([[campaign/quest-rewards]]).

**Post-31 / mapping setup [ME]**
- Ice Shot: Elemental Armament II, Ice Bite II, Rapid Attacks II, Fork.
- Snipe: Elemental Armament II, Concentrated Area, Elemental Focus, Window of Opportunity II, Close Combat II.
- Herald of Ice: Magnified Area II, Elemental Armament II, Far Combat II, Added Cold Damage.
- Also: Tornado Shot, Ice-Tipped Arrows, Freezing Mark, Barrage, Combat Frenzy, Mirage Archer (full lists in [ME]).
- 6-link priority: Snipe, Ice Shot, Herald of Ice, Freezing Mark. Weapon set 1 = damage skills; set 2 = Freezing Mark.
- Requirement: 45 Str / 75 Dex / 50 Int for the supports.
- Boss loop: pre-place Tornado Shot, Ice-Tipped Arrows, spam Ice Shot until frozen, Barrage into Snipe with Tornado Shot still active. 1.0 risk: freeze rules may change ([[mechanics/chill-freeze]]).

**Spirit-gem gold play (dated):** poe.ninja, Forbidden Rites, 2026-11-17: L8 spirit ≈12.2 div, L10 ≈0.44, L12 ≈0.50 ([[strategy/league-lifecycle-pricing]]). L10 needs char 36, L12 needs 46 ([[gems/gem-levels]]). Which campaign source gives an L8 spirit gem isn't in the KB — check the exchange the moment you see one.

## 2. Ascendancy
- Order [ML][ME]: Projectile Proximity Specialisation (Point Blank) → Endless Munitions → Gathering Winds → Mirage Deadeye.
- Points: 2 per trial step ([[classes/ascendancy-trials]]). Act 2 quest Sekhemas (Balbala's Barya, 1 floor) = points 1–2. Act 3 Trial of Chaos quest (key from Xyclucian) = points 3–4. Typical campaign finish = 4/8.

## 3. Passives
- Attributes: default Dex; fix gear requirements with attribute amulets or a Str belt [ML].
- [ML] shows 123 main + 20 per weapon set; layout unclear → check the in-game planner. KB says 24 weapon-set points come from quests ([[classes/weapon-set-points]]). Open item.
- Mapping tree is a level-75 tree; enter maps ~60–65 and fill as you go. Need defence → evasion/deflection nodes, else damage [ME].

## 4. Gear breakpoints
- Act 1: flat attack damage first. Vendor-check gloves every 1–2 levels [ML]. Shortbow from Red Vale, be level 5 before Rust King. Transmute + augment bow and quiver.
- **L11:** Warden Bow (drop or vendor, best DPS). Spend no crafting currency [ML].
- **L16:** craft a 6-mod Recurve with 2 sockets. Save Artificer's, Exalted, Regal, Alchemy [ML]. Loop: good magic base → essence/regal → exalts to 3 prefixes → runes → whetstones. Rune order Storm > Desert > Glacial > Iron ([[builds/levelling-gear]]).
- **L28 / L33:** repeat on Dualstring (28), Cultist (33). Later bases: Zealot 39, Artillery 45 ([[builds/levelling-gear]]).
- Quiver: added levels + high flat damage. Boots: movement speed always; only swap for other MS boots. Other slots: evasion or evasion/ES; life + res off non-damage slots; belt Str; helmet Dex/Int [ML].
- Boots MS tiers by ilvl: 10% (1), 15% (16), 20% (33), 25% (46), 30% (65) ([[builds/levelling-gear]]).
- Flasks: replace at 10, 16, 23, 30, 40, 50, 60 [ML]. Grand flasks early Act 2 (Act 1 vendors cap at ilvl 15; 0.2-era Maxroll).
- Salvage quality weapons → Whetstones; socketed items → Artificer's Orbs [ML]. Whetstones ≈6.5 ex late-league (2026-11-17) — check price before using.

## 5. Act checklists
Format: zone (level) → what to take. "pts" = weapon-set passive points. Bold = ML says don't skip.

### Act 1 (areas 1–15)
- Order [ML]: Devourer (Mud Burrow 3), Beira (Clearfell 2), Areagne (Grelwood 4), Rust King (Red Vale 5).
- Take: Beira +10% cold res · Crowbell 2 pts (Hunting Grounds 10) · King in the Mists +30 spirit (Freythorn 11) · Una's Lute 2 pts (Ogham Farmlands) · Candlemass +20 life (Ogham Manor 15) · Salvage Bench (Ogham Village).
- Currency: Cemetery Regal · Tomb of the Consort random amulet · Hunting Grounds Exalt · Manor Ramparts L5 gem (optional) · **Ogham Manor Alchemy** (for the L16 bow) [ML].
- Resist check: cold 30–40% for Count Ogham (Maxroll, 0.2-era; [[builds/levelling-gear]]).

### Act 2 (16–31)
- Take: Vastiri Outskirts Exalt · **Mastodon Lesser Jeweller's** · Kabala 2 pts (Keth 21) · Dekhara letter 2 pts (Deshar) · +10% lightning res (Spires of Deshar 30, easy to miss) · Valley of the Titans relic/medallion choice (sources disagree, [[campaign/act-2]]; [ML] says +1 charm slot, 30% charge) · Balbala's Barya → Sekhemas (ascendancy 1–2).
- Fire res before Rudja (Mawdun Mine 18) [Maxroll, 0.2-era].

### Act 3 (33–45)
- Take: Silverfist 2 pts (Jungle Ruins 34) · Venom Vial (Venom Crypts) → [ML] 25% stun threshold · Blackjaw +10% fire res (Machinarium 37) · Ignagduk +30 spirit (Azak Bog 40) · Reforging Bench (Mektul, Molten Vault 41) · Sacrificial Heart 2 pts (chance drop, can eat time [INFERRED]).
- Xyclucian (Chimeral Wetlands 36) → Inscribed Ultimatum → Trial of Chaos (ascendancy 3–4).
- Fire res before Azak Bog boss [Maxroll, 0.2-era]. Start gambling rares for gold-sold upgrades here [ML].

### Act 4 (46–53)
- Take: **Ngakanu Greater Jeweller's** (Heart of the Tribe 53) · Journey's End: Hartlin gives L13 skill gem + Delirium drop, Omniphobia 2 pts · Halls of the Dead: Yama 2 pts + tattoo (+5 Dex or +5% res; [ML] lists Dex and cold/fire res) · Navali 2 pts (Trial of the Ancestors) · Eye of Hinekora +5% mana · Abandoned Prison +30% flask life recovery · Whakapanu shark → +30% evasion [ML] (Kaom/Rakiata choice, [[campaign/act-4]]).
- Which Act 4 bosses give the points is disputed → [[04-conflicts]].

### Interludes (0.5.5 only, removed in 1.0)
- [ML]: Khari Crossing +5% max life and +3% MS · Kriar Village +40 spirit · Interlude choice (res / MS / global defence, changeable) · Eye of Hinekora/Halls etc. as above.

## 6. Totals to hit by campaign end (0.5.x)
- Spirit 100 (30 + 30 + 40). Endgame amulet needs ≥30 spirit to run all spirit gems [ME].
- Res: +10% each element (Beira, Spires, Blackjaw) + tattoo. Cap 75% after the −60% campaign penalty ≈135% uncapped ([[builds/defence-checklist]]). Chaos 30–50% before red maps.
- Defence gate: Deadeye is low on early defence — note the tier where it fails ([[builds/starter-archetypes]]).

## 7. After the campaign [ME]
Non-crit setup with Heart of the Well → strong crit bow + quiver → big evasion chest → ~450 ES helmet → boots/gloves/rings/amulet → Against the Darkness. Bow: Obliterator. Early variant is non-crit, life/evasion, SSF-friendly.
