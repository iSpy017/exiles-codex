## What 1.0 changes for starters (official)
@ v=1.0 vol=may-change conf=confirmed date=2026-11-17
GGG names major reworks to Mercenary, Infernalist, Blood Mage and Acolyte of Chayula; the passive tree gets a massive overhaul (jewel sockets moved into clusters); support gems get rebalanced; 70+ uniques reworked, 40+ new. Duelist (Knight, Master-at-Arms) and Shadow (Assassin, Infiltrator) are new.
- src: GGG press release, 2026-11-11 [S017], tier 1; [[classes/index]]; retrieved 2026-11-17
- v: 1.0 (announcement, not patch notes)
- conf: confirmed (tier 1)
- notes: Deadeye, Pathfinder, Martial Artist, Oracle, Shaman, the Sorceress and Warrior ascendancies are not named in the rework list → [INFERRED] lower rework risk, but the tree and support changes hit everyone. Secondhand reveal notes also say ailments become harder to apply but stronger and attack times scale with attack speed (claude/reveal-notes-and-price-findings.md, unverified).
