# League Starter Archetypes

Version: 0.5 / 0.5.5 tier lists + 1.0 announcements (researched 2026-11-17). Volatility: volatile (meta). Rebuild on 1.0 patch-notes day — every class gets the passive-tree overhaul and support rebalance.

## What makes a league starter (sourced criteria)
- Claim: Tier-list makers judge starters on campaign and mapping speed, boss damage, movement and crowd control, early defences, clear speed, and how well it works with little or no trade (SSF / very low budget).
- Version: 0.5–0.5.5
- Volatility: may-change
- Source: [Mobalytics 0.5 League Starter Tier List](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/tier-list), tier 3; [Kami-labs summary of Fubgun's 0.5.5 tier list](https://kami-labs.fr/en/path-of-exile-2/tier-list-league-start-poe2-0-5-5/) (2026-09-02; SSF/low-budget framing), tier 5 summarising a tier-3 creator; retrieved 2026-11-17
- Confidence: confirmed
- Notes: David's own weights and gates → claude/starter-decision-sheet.md; the "rental car" idea and 5-div T15 test → [[strategy/league-start-framework]].

## What 1.0 changes for starters (official)
- Claim: GGG names major reworks to Mercenary, Infernalist, Blood Mage and Acolyte of Chayula; the passive tree gets a massive overhaul (jewel sockets moved into clusters); support gems get rebalanced; 70+ uniques reworked, 40+ new. Duelist (Knight, Master-at-Arms) and Shadow (Assassin, Infiltrator) are new.
- Version: 1.0 (announcement, not patch notes)
- Volatility: may-change
- Source: [GGG press release, 2026-11-11](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/4013917/page/1), tier 1; [[classes/index]]; retrieved 2026-11-17
- Confidence: confirmed (tier 1)
- Notes: Deadeye, Pathfinder, Martial Artist, Oracle, Shaman, the Sorceress and Warrior ascendancies are not named in the rework list → [INFERRED] lower rework risk, but the tree and support changes hit everyone. Secondhand reveal notes also say ailments become harder to apply but stronger and attack times scale with attack speed (claude/reveal-notes-and-price-findings.md, unverified).
