## What's announced
@ v=1.0 vol=may-change conf=confirmed date=2026-11-17
Act 6 is the final act of the six-act 1.0 campaign. In May 2026 GGG said most Act 5 environment work was done and a little was left for Act 6. GGG's director was "coy" about whether the finale is exactly one act ("more than one act").

- src: FRVR interview report [S022], tier 3; PCGamesN interview [S660], tier 3; Maketecheasier — Steam announcement [S030], tier 3; retrieved 2026-11-17
- v: 1.0 (announced)
- conf: confirmed that Acts 5 and 6 ship in 1.0 (multiple sources, incl. Steam announcement); content unknown

## Official counts (Acts 5 + 6 combined)
@ v=1.0 vol=may-change conf=confirmed date=2026-11-23
24 distinct areas, 18 new multi-phase unique bosses and 20+ Champion Rare encounters across Acts 5 and 6. The story ends the Twilight Order and the Beast "once and for all".

- v: 1.0 (announced)
