## Official counts (Acts 5 + 6 combined)
@ v=1.0 vol=may-change conf=confirmed date=2026-11-23
Acts 5 and 6 together have 24 distinct areas, 18 new multi-phase unique bosses and 20+ new Champion Rare encounters. Oriath has been "ravaged by countless disasters". Named area types: twisted slums, opulent estates, the Temple District, the Grand Arena. The story wraps up the Twilight Order and the Beast.
