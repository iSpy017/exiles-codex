# Permanent campaign rewards (0.5.x totals)

Per-act detail → [[act-1]], [[act-2]], [[act-3]]; Act 4 and Interludes summarised here until their own docs (batch 12). All 0.5 — 1.0 removes the Interludes and adds Acts 5–6, so totals will change.

## Totals (0.5)
- Claim: Full campaign gives 24 weapon-set passive points, 100 spirit, +10% each to fire/cold/lightning res, +20 life and 5% max life, 5% max mana, choice rewards, Salvage + Reforging benches.
- Version: 0.5.x
- Volatility: may-change (1.0 restructure)
- Source: [Mobalytics 0.5 campaign checklist](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/0-5-campaign-checklist-stats-rewards-skillpoints-and-more) (updated 2026-06-02), tier 3; [PoE2DB Act 1–3](https://poe2db.tw/us/Act_1), tier 2; Buyboost 0.5 checklist, tier 5; retrieved 2026-11-17
- Confidence: confirmed (tier 3 + tier 2 per act + tier 5 totals agree)
- Notes: Older totals (+20% all res, +8% life, 26 points, 130 spirit) come from the early-access Normal + Cruel campaign, where rewards repeated in Cruel ([Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/getting-started/permanent-stats-from-campaign), Sportskeeda). Not a conflict — different version. Passive points: up to 99 from levels + 24 from quests = 123 ([poe2wiki Passive Skill Tree](https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Passive_Skill_Points), tier 2).

## By act (0.5)
- Claim:
  - Act 1: 4 points (Crowbell, Una's Lute), 30 spirit (King in the Mists), 10% cold res (Beira), 20 life (Candlemass), Salvage Bench.
  - Act 2: 4 points (Kabala, Dekhara's letter), 10% lightning res (Sisters of Garukhan), relic-altar choice.
  - Act 3: 4 points (Silverfist, Sacrificial Heart), 30 spirit (Ignagduk), 10% fire res (Blackjaw), venom-vial threshold/regen choice, Reforging Bench.
  - Act 4: 4 points (Omniphobia, Yama the White), 5% max mana, Kaom's/Rakiata's Lesson choice, a tattoo choice (+5 attribute or +5% one res), flask-recovery choice.
  - Interludes: 6 points (Akthi & Anundr, Abominable Yeti, Oswin) + 2 points from the Hooded One after all three, 40 spirit (Lythara), 5% max life (Molten Shrine), Qimah pillar choice.
- Version: 0.5.x
- Volatility: may-change
- Source: [Mobalytics 0.5 campaign checklist](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/0-5-campaign-checklist-stats-rewards-skillpoints-and-more), tier 3; [PoE2DB Act 1](https://poe2db.tw/us/Act_1)/[2](https://poe2db.tw/us/Act_2)/[3](https://poe2db.tw/us/Act_3), tier 2; [poe2wiki Quest reward](https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Quest_reward) (Lythara +40 spirit, Yeti +2), tier 2; retrieved 2026-11-17
- Confidence: confirmed for Acts 1–3 (two sources); Act 4 / Interludes mostly single tier-3
- Notes: Mobalytics' own per-act summaries add to 20 points, not 24 — its tables add to 24 (flagged on the page). Spirit: 30 + 30 + 40 = 100 matches [[spirit-reservation]]. Weapon-set points → [[weapon-set-points]].
