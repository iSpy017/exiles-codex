## Status in 1.0: removed
@ v=1.0 vol=may-change conf=confirmed date=2026-11-17
The three Interludes (temporary side stories between Act 4 and the endgame) are removed in 1.0, replaced by Acts 5 and 6. GGG says their areas, bosses and other elements will be reused in other forms.
- src: PCGamesN interview with Jonathan Rogers [S660], tier 3 (developer quote); Niche Gamer — Third Edict announcement [S101] (GGG said the Interludes go away when Acts 5–6 arrive), tier 3; retrieved 2026-11-17
- v: 1.0 (announced)
- vol: may-change until patch notes
- conf: confirmed (two sources quoting GGG)
- notes: Status → retired at the 1.0 refresh. Rewards below (8 points, 40 spirit, 5% life) must come from somewhere else in 1.0 if totals stay — check Acts 5–6.


## What they were (0.5 reference)
@ v=0.5.5.9 vol=retired conf=confirmed date=2026-11-17
Three chains at area level 54–56, each with its own town (The Refuge, The Khari Bazaar, The Glade): Interlude 1 "The Stolen Barya" (Khari: Khari Crossing, Pools of Khatal, Sel Khari Sanctuary, Galai Gates, Qimah, Qimah Reservoir), Interlude 2 "Doryani's Contingency" (Ashen Forest, Kriar Village, Glacial Tarn, Howling Caves, Kriar Peaks, Etched Ravine, Cuachic Vault), Interlude 3 "The Curse of Holten" (Scorched Farmlands, Stones of Serle, Blackwood, Holten, Wolvenhold, Holten Estate).
- src: RePoE world_areas.json [S002] (P1_/P2_/P3_ ids, act 6 tag), tier 2; Mobalytics 0.5 campaign checklist [S049] (chain names), tier 3; retrieved 2026-11-17
- vol: retired in 1.0
- notes: Assigning zones to chains by id prefix is [INFERRED] (P2 = Khari zones, P3 = Kriar zones, P1 = Holten zones).
