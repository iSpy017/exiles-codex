## Routing principles (guides)
@ v=0.5 vol=may-change conf=confirmed date=2026-11-17
Skip optional content that doesn't make you permanently stronger; do permanent-reward quests and bosses; avoid full-clearing; low-XP early zones (Riverbank, Clearfell, Grelwood) can be skipped through quickly; kill magic packs, skip white mobs. Save early currency for the level-16 and level-28/33 weapon upgrades.
- src: Mobalytics campaign XP tips (Guythatdies) [S095], tier 3; Mobalytics league-mechanic reward sheet [S074], tier 3; ExpCarry 0.4.0d guide, tier 5; retrieved 2026-11-17
- v: 0.4–0.5
- conf: confirmed (tier 3 + tier 5 agree)
- notes: Conflict of approach, not of facts: one tier-5 guide warns that skipping boss kills is the main cause of missing support gems (guaranteed quest gems). For the super-sweat route: take gem-reward bosses that are on the path [INFERRED].


## XP penalty
@ v=0.5.x vol=may-change conf=single-source date=2026-11-17
Full XP within 3 + (character level ÷ 16) levels of the area level.
- src: Mobalytics endgame power levelling [S065], tier 3, retrieved 2026-11-17
- notes: Don't over-level ahead of zones; see [[item-level]].
