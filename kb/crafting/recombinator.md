# Recombinator (removed in 0.5)

## Status: removed
- Claim: The Recombinator was removed in 0.5 (Return of the Ancients). Before that it was an Expedition crafting device that merged two items of the same class: you picked mods from each, the shown success chance fell as you picked more mods, and failure destroyed both items. Omen of Recombination made the success roll lucky.
- Version: existed 0.2–0.4; removed 0.5.0
- Volatility: may-change (could return in 1.0)
- Source: [Mobalytics Expedition crafting](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/crafting-expedition), tier 3; [Vulkk 0.5.0 changes overview](https://vulkk.com/2026/05/22/path-of-exile-2-patch-0-5-0-changes-overview/), tier 3; [poe2wiki Recombinator](https://poe2wiki.net/wiki/Recombinator), tier 2 (mechanics); [Mobalytics Omen of Recombination](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/omen-of-recombination), tier 3; Boostmatch 0.5 guide, tier 5; retrieved 2026-11-17
- Confidence: confirmed (removal: two tier-3 sources)
- Notes: Omen of Recombination is still in the 0.5.5.9 game data (drop level 75, no description) — [INFERRED] leftover entry, since Boostmatch (tier 5) says it was deleted on patch day. Boostmatch also says the Expedition vendors (Rog, Tujen, Gwennen) no longer appear in 0.5 — tier 5 only; verify in endgame/expedition. Re-check at the 1.0 refresh.
