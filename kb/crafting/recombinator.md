## Status: removed
@ v=0.5.0 vol=may-change conf=confirmed date=2026-11-17
The Recombinator was removed in 0.5 (Return of the Ancients). Before that it was an Expedition crafting device that merged two items of the same class: you picked mods from each, the shown success chance fell as you picked more mods, and failure destroyed both items. Omen of Recombination made the success roll lucky.
